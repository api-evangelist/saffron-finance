DeFi has come a long way in the past few years — we’ve seen more participants, more protocols, more capital, and (naturally) more scrutiny. But for all the innovation we’ve seen in decentralized markets, there’s still one major component that hasn’t caught up to the rest of the industry: insurance. When it comes to traditional finance, insurance isn’t optional — it’s the foundation of the market that makes everything else investable to a wider demographic.
