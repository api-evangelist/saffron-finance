It’s 2025 and safety doesn’t look like it used to. As I elaborated on in my previous article , uncertainty is currently plaguing the market — the dollar remains strong versus other fiat currents, but bonds are hardly outpacing inflation, and there’s anticipation that we’ll see further rate cuts in the near future. It feels like over the past few years, parking part of your portfolio in cash doesn’t feel as secure as it used to.
