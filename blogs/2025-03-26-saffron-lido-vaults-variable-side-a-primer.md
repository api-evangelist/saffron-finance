Saffron Lido Vaults split ETH staking yield into fixed and variable rates. Users on the fixed side earn all of their yield up front, and users on the variable side take on more risk to potentially multiply their yield. This article explains how the Variable Side works and walks through an example scenario to help readers see it in action.
