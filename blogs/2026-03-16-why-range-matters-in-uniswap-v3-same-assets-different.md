Before Uniswap v3, providing liquidity was simple — you deposited two assets into a pool and your capital was spread evenly across all possible prices. Whether ETH traded at $500 or $5,000, your liquidity stayed active collecting fees the entire time. As a result your liquidity was distributed across the entire price curve of the pair, only a small portion of your capital was actually being used at the current trading price at any moment.
